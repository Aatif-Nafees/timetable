Food
▾

300–350ml hot water at 80–85°C (stop before rolling boil). Add all ingredients, steep 5–7 mins, strain and drink.

Ingredients

½ tsp jeera  ·  ½ tsp saunf  ·  ¼ tsp ajwain  ·  ¼ tsp haldi  ·  2–3 cracks black pepper  ·  ¼ tsp cinnamon
